Privacy Policy

Irodoku · Last updated: August 1, 2026

Irodoku (“the App”) is a color-based Sudoku puzzle game. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Summary

Irodoku does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress and settings stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device (for example via on-device preferences storage):

  • Game settings (difficulty, color palette, sound, dark mode, chromatic mode)
  • Gameplay progress (current or paused puzzle state)
  • Local statistics (wins, streaks, best times)
  • Achievement unlocks and related counters

This data is used only to run the App and remember your preferences. It is not uploaded to our servers.

Information we do not collect

  • No account or login is required
  • No name, email, phone number, or payment information
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party ads
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs in the App
  • No location data

Internet access

The App does not require an internet connection to play. If you use the web version hosted on GitHub Pages, standard web hosting and browser behavior may apply (for example, your browser requesting the page from GitHub’s servers). That hosting is separate from in-app gameplay data, which remains local.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to delete upon request beyond what you can clear by uninstalling the App or clearing app/browser storage.

Your choices

You can reset or remove locally stored data by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, clearing site data in your browser (web version), or uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent by opening an issue on the project repository: github.com/anthonyraad/Irodoku/issues
