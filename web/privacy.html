Privacy Policy

Irodoku · Last updated: August 2, 2026

Irodoku (“the App”) is a color-based Sudoku puzzle game. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Summary

Irodoku does not sell or share personal information and has no ads or tracking. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Online features (Daily leaderboards, Graffiti multiplayer, optional progress sync, and optional Discord linking) use an anonymous Firebase player ID and the name you choose. On Android, on-device data may also be included in your device’s Google backup if you have backup enabled.

Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device (for example via on-device preferences storage):

  • Game settings (difficulty, color palette, sound, dark mode, chromatic mode)
  • Gameplay progress (current or paused puzzle state)
  • Local statistics (wins, streaks, best times)
  • Achievement unlocks and related counters

This data is used to run the App and remember your preferences. It stays on your device unless you use the online features described below.

Android device backup

On Android, the App opts into the system Auto Backup / device-transfer features so your on-device game data (settings, statistics, achievements, and related progress) can be backed up with your Google account’s device backup, if you have that enabled in system settings. Restore typically happens when you set up a new device or restore the App from backup. This is handled by the Android system and your Google account backup settings—not by Irodoku operating its own cloud account or servers. You can disable device backup in your Android system settings.

Online features

The App signs in to Google Firebase anonymously (no email or password) to get a random player ID. With that ID we store, in Firebase Realtime Database: your Daily Iro / Daily times with the display name you set and the time of completion (shown publicly on the Daily leaderboards); Graffiti multiplayer room data; and, if you choose to sync progress with Google, your game progress linked to that Google account.

Information we do not collect

  • No account is required; online play uses an anonymous player ID
  • No email, phone number, or payment information (beyond the display name you choose)
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party ads
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs in the App
  • No location data

Internet access

The App does not require an internet connection to play. Leaderboards, Graffiti multiplayer, progress sync, and Discord linking need internet access and use Google Firebase and, for Discord linking, a Cloudflare Worker operated by us. If you use the web version hosted on GitHub Pages, standard web hosting and browser behavior may apply.

Linking Discord (optional)

On Android you can choose Sync Discord in Settings. If you link your Discord account, we store your Discord user ID and Discord display name, linked to the App’s anonymous player ID, so we can show your Daily Iro leaderboard entry under that name and sync your Daily Iro progress between the App and the Discord Activity. We only request Discord’s basic “identify” permission. You can unlink at any time with Disconnect in Settings, which removes the link.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online features store only an anonymous player ID, the display name you choose, scores, and (if linked) a Discord ID and name. Contact us to have leaderboard entries removed.

Your choices

You can reset or remove locally stored data by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, clearing site data in your browser (web version), or uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent by opening an issue on the project repository: github.com/anthonyraad/Irodoku/issues
